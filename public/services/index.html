Repair and maintenance

Diagnosis first, then a quote. Nothing gets replaced without your OK.

  • Oil changes and maintenance

    Oil and filter, fluid top-offs and the factory maintenance items due at your mileage.

    • Conventional or synthetic
    • Tire pressure set
    • Multi-point check
    Request this service for Oil changes and maintenance

  • Brakes

    Pads, rotors, calipers and brake fluid. We measure what's left and show you before replacing anything.

    • Pad and rotor measurements
    • Fluid condition test
    • Test drive after
    Request this service for Brakes

  • Check engine and diagnostics

    Code reading plus the testing that finds the actual failed part, not just the code.

    • Scan and freeze-frame data
    • Pinpoint testing
    • Repair quoted before work
    Request this service for Check engine and diagnostics

  • Steering and suspension

    Clunks, pulling and worn parts: struts, shocks, tie rods, ball joints and wheel bearings.

    • Road test
    • Component inspection
    • Alignment referral or service
    Request this service for Steering and suspension

  • Batteries and electrical

    Batteries, alternators, starters and the wiring problems that cause no-starts.

    • Charging system test
    • Battery replacement
    • Parasitic draw testing
    Request this service for Batteries and electrical

  • Heating and A/C

    No heat, no cold air, or a fan that won't quit. Diagnosed and repaired.

    • Leak check
    • Recharge
    • Blower and blend door repair
    Request this service for Heating and A/C

Detailing

Interior and exterior packages, quoted by vehicle size and condition.

  • Interior detailing

    Vacuum, shampoo, steam and wipe-down of every surface, with leather cleaned and conditioned.

    • Seats and carpet shampoo
    • Vents and crevices
    • Glass inside
    Request this service for Interior detailing

  • Exterior wash and protection

    Hand wash, decontamination, and a sealant or wax that keeps water beading for months.

    • Hand wash and dry
    • Clay bar decontamination
    • Sealant or wax
    Request this service for Exterior wash and protection

  • Paint correction

    Machine polishing to reduce swirls, haze and light scratches before protection.

    • Paint depth checked
    • One- or two-step polish
    • Finish inspection
    Request this service for Paint correction

  • Wheels and tires

    Brake dust removed, wheels sealed and tires dressed.

    • Barrels and faces
    • Iron decontamination
    • Tire dressing
    Request this service for Wheels and tires
